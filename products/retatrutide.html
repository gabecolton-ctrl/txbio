Not for Human or Animal Consumption:

This product is sold exclusively for laboratory research use by qualified professionals and institutions. Research Overview

Retatrutide is a triple-agonist peptide studied in metabolic and endocrine research settings, most commonly in in-vitro and preclinical models investigating receptor activity and energy metabolism pathways.

Handling & Storage

Store lyophilized powder at -20°C. Once reconstituted, refrigerate at 2-8°C and use within the timeframe indicated by your lab protocol.
