Research overview

Semaglutide is a synthetic analog of glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1). The lysine at position 20 carries a C18 fatty diacid on a γ-glutamate and two-OEG linker, which binds serum albumin and greatly extends its half-life, and an Aib substitution at position 2 protects it from DPP-4 cleavage. It is used in research on GLP-1 receptor signaling, glucose homeostasis, and appetite and energy-balance models.

Research areas

  • GLP-1 receptor pharmacology
  • Glucose and insulin regulation
  • Appetite and energy-balance models
  • Long-acting peptide design
