Not for Human or Animal Consumption:

This product is sold exclusively for laboratory research use by qualified professionals and institutions. It is not a drug, food, cosmetic, or dietary supplement, and is not approved for use in humans or animals. Handling must be performed only by trained personnel in a controlled research setting.

Research Overview

Tesamorelin is a synthetic peptide analog studied for its interaction with growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) receptors in endocrine and metabolic research. This is a higher-concentration pack size for larger research protocols.

Handling & Storage

Store lyophilized powder at -20°C. Once reconstituted, refrigerate at 2-8°C and use within the timeframe indicated by your lab protocol.
