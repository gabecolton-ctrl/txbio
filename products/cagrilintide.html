Research overview

Cagrilintide is a long-acting, acylated analog of the hormone amylin. It binds the amylin 3 receptor and the calcitonin receptor with picomolar affinity and has a low tendency to form amyloid fibrils. In rodent studies it reduced food intake for at least 48 hours after a single dose and reduced body weight and fat mass in diet-induced obesity models. It has also been studied in combination with semaglutide.

Research areas

  • Amylin and calcitonin receptor pharmacology
  • Satiety and food-intake models
  • Combination studies with GLP-1 agonists
