Research overview

Melanotan I (afamelanotide) is a synthetic 13-amino-acid analog of alpha-melanocyte-stimulating hormone (α-MSH) that binds mainly the MC1 receptor. As afamelanotide it was approved in the EU in 2015 and the US in 2019 for patients with erythropoietic protoporphyria.

Research areas

  • MC1 receptor pharmacology
  • Melanogenesis
  • Photoprotection research
