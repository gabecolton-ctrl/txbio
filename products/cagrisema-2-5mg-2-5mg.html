Research overview

This vial combines Cagrilintide and Semaglutide (2.5mg cagrilintide + 2.5mg semaglutide per vial).

Cagrilintide

Cagrilintide is a long-acting, acylated analog of the hormone amylin. It binds the amylin 3 receptor and the calcitonin receptor with picomolar affinity and has a low tendency to form amyloid fibrils. In rodent studies it reduced food intake for at least 48 hours after a single dose and reduced body weight and fat mass in diet-induced obesity models. It has also been studied in combination with semaglutide.

Semaglutide

Semaglutide is a synthetic analog of glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1). The lysine at position 20 carries a C18 fatty diacid on a γ-glutamate and two-OEG linker, which binds serum albumin and greatly extends its half-life, and an Aib substitution at position 2 protects it from DPP-4 cleavage. It is used in research on GLP-1 receptor signaling, glucose homeostasis, and appetite and energy-balance models.
