Research overview

This vial combines BPC-157 and TB-500 (15mg BPC-157 + 15mg TB-500 per vial).

BPC-157

BPC-157 is a synthetic 15-amino-acid peptide derived from a protein found in human gastric juice. It has been studied mainly in laboratory animals, in models of tendon, muscle, and gastrointestinal injury. Human data are very limited, it is not approved by any drug regulatory agency, and the World Anti-Doping Agency has prohibited it since 2022.

TB-500

TB-500 is a synthetic, N-acetylated heptapeptide matching residues 17–23 of thymosin β4, the actin-binding region of that protein. In laboratory studies it inhibited the proliferation and migration of stimulated hepatic stellate cells, and topical application shortened wound-closure times in mice. It has not undergone human trials, and the World Anti-Doping Agency prohibits it.
