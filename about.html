About Texas Advanced BioResearch

TXBioResearch is a Dallas-based supplier of research peptides, giving laboratories and qualified researchers dependable access to well-documented materials for scientific study.

What we do

We source and distribute peptide compounds intended exclusively for laboratory research, including in-vitro studies, assay development, and academic research programs.

Our catalog spans metabolic research peptides (such as Retatrutide and Tesamorelin), growth hormone secretagogues (Ipamorelin), growth factor peptides (IGF-1), neuropeptides (Semax), mitochondrial-derived peptides (MOTS-c), combination research blends (GLOW, KLOW), and standard laboratory reagents including NAD+, glutathione, and bacteriostatic water for reconstitution.

Our standards

Sourcing

We work with manufacturers who provide documentation supporting research-grade quality, with purity specs listed on each product page.

Handling and storage

Products are shipped and stored under standard laboratory-chemical handling practices, including cold-chain requirements where applicable.

Buyer verification

Buyers register an account and accept research-use terms before viewing full pricing or completing any order.

Intended use

Every product listed on this site is sold strictly for laboratory research use by qualified professionals. Products are not intended for human or animal consumption, injection, or any therapeutic, diagnostic, or cosmetic use. TXBioResearch does not sell to the general public for personal use. Full catalog access requires account registration, and all buyers confirm their research intent both at signup and at checkout.

Our commitment

We take compliance seriously. TXBioResearch makes no claims about the safety, efficacy, or suitability of our products for human or animal use, and we do not provide dosing, administration, or usage guidance of any kind, because our products are not intended for those purposes.

Any research conducted with our materials is the sole responsibility of the purchasing laboratory or institution, which is expected to follow all applicable safety protocols and regulatory requirements.
