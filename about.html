About Texas Advanced BioResearch

Texas Advanced BioResearch (TXBioResearch) is a Dallas, TX-based supplier of research peptides, providing laboratories and qualified researchers with materials for scientific investigation.

We source and distribute peptide compounds intended exclusively for laboratory research applications, including in-vitro studies, assay development, and academic research programs. Our catalog spans metabolic peptides, growth factor compounds, neuropeptides, and standard laboratory reagents, and is built to support researchers who need consistent access to well-documented research materials.

Intended Use:

Every product listed on this site is sold strictly for laboratory research use by qualified professionals. Products are not intended for human or animal consumption, injection, or any therapeutic, diagnostic, or cosmetic use. TXBioResearch does not sell to the general public for personal use. Full catalog access requires account registration, and all buyers are required to confirm their research intent both at signup and at checkout.

Our Standards

Sourcing

We work with manufacturers who provide documentation supporting research-grade quality, with purity specs listed on each product page.

Handling & Storage

Products are shipped and stored in accordance with standard laboratory-chemical handling practices, including cold-chain requirements where applicable.

Buyer Verification

We require buyers to register an account and acknowledge research-use terms before viewing full pricing or completing any order.

Our Product Categories

Our current catalog includes metabolic research peptides (such as Retatrutide and Tesamorelin), growth hormone secretagogues (Ipamorelin), growth factor peptides (IGF-1), neuropeptides (Semax), mitochondrial-derived peptides (MOTS-c), combination research blends (GLOW, KLOW), and standard laboratory reagents including NAD+, glutathione, and bacteriostatic water for reconstitution.

Our Commitment

We take compliance seriously. TXBioResearch does not make any claims about the safety, efficacy, or suitability of our products for human or animal use, and we do not provide dosing, administration, or usage guidance of any kind, because our products are not intended for those purposes. Any research conducted with our materials is the sole responsibility of the purchasing laboratory or institution, which is expected to follow all applicable safety protocols and regulatory requirements.
